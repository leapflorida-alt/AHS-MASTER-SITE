DEPLOYMENT:
1. Log into Netlify.
2. Open the site deployment area.
3. Drag the ZIP file into the manual deploy area.
4. Netlify will publish index.html automatically.

The estimate form uses Netlify Forms. After deployment, test one form submission.

Before final launch, confirm the company phone number, email, license/insurance wording, business hours, and review claims.
