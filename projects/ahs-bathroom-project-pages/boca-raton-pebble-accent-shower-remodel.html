Project Overview

Designed for calm, comfort and everyday use.

This Boca Raton shower renovation pairs clean horizontal lines with organic pebble textures for a calm, spa-inspired result.

Warm gray wood-look wall tile gives the enclosure a tailored contemporary foundation. A vertical pebble mosaic accent aligns with the controls and continues across the shower floor, adding natural texture and visual movement. Frameless glass preserves the open feeling and keeps attention on the detailed tile installation.
