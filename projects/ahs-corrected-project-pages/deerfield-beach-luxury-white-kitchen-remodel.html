Project Overview

Designed with purpose. Finished with restraint.

This Deerfield Beach kitchen remodel blends timeless cabinetry with modern lighting and clean, uninterrupted surfaces.

Full-height white shaker cabinetry creates a custom built-in appearance, while the quartz countertop and matching full-height backsplash keep the room bright and visually seamless. Integrated under-cabinet and ceiling lighting adds a soft architectural glow. Brass plumbing details provide warmth, and the oversized island expands preparation space while supporting casual seating and entertaining.

BacksplashFull-height quartz slab backsplash

Design Highlights

Distinctive details shaped around daily life.

Architectural Lighting

Concealed cove lighting traces the upper cabinetry and ceiling, giving the room a polished evening glow.

Full-Height Cabinetry

Tall custom cabinetry maximizes storage while creating the appearance of integrated millwork.

Quartz Continuity

Matching quartz counters and backsplash reduce visual clutter and make the kitchen feel more expansive.

Brass Details

A warm brass pot filler and complementary accents soften the cool white palette.

Integrated Appliances

The refrigerator and cooking zone are framed by cabinetry for a more intentional built-in look.

Oversized Island

A large quartz island increases work area and supports everyday gathering.
