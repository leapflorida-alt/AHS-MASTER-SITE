Roofing questions

Helpful answers before an estimate.

Is a leak always a sign that I need a new roof?

No. Some leaks are localized and repairable, while others reveal broader underlayment or system failure. Roof age, previous repairs and the extent of damage all influence the recommendation.

Can you inspect a roof after a storm?

We can discuss visible storm-related concerns and project availability. If there is an active emergency, protect people and property first and contact the appropriate emergency or insurance resources.

What affects roof replacement cost?

Roof size, slope, access, covering type, deck condition, flashing, penetrations, disposal and code-related requirements all affect the final scope.

Concerned about your roof?

Send your property address, roof type, approximate age and photos of the concern.
