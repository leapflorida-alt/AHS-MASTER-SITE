Project Overview

A compact bathroom made to feel open, calm and modern.

This South Florida bathroom renovation features large-format gray tile, a frameless glass partition, a rainfall shower and a recessed accent niche.

Using the same tile across the floor and shower walls creates a continuous, modern composition. The clear glass panel keeps the compact room visually open, while dark floating shelves add contrast and practical storage. A handheld shower and overhead rain fixture provide flexibility without cluttering the design.
