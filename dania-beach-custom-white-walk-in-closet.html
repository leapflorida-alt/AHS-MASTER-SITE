Project Overview

A place for everything, without crowding the room.

This Dania Beach walk-in closet was organized with bright white built-ins, adjustable shelving, hanging zones and integrated drawers.

The symmetrical layout creates clear storage on both sides while preserving an open center aisle. Multiple hanging heights accommodate different wardrobe pieces, and the rear drawer tower adds concealed storage without taking away from the room's light, streamlined appearance.
