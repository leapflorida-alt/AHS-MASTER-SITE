Irrigation questions

Common signs and next steps.

Why does one zone have low pressure?

Leaks, damaged heads, valve problems, restrictions or too many outlets on a zone can all reduce pressure. Running and observing the zone helps isolate the likely cause.

Can you reduce water spraying onto pavement?

Often, yes. Head alignment, nozzle selection, placement and run times can be reviewed to reduce overspray while maintaining reasonable landscape coverage.

Should irrigation be checked after exterior construction?

Yes. New concrete, patios, additions and landscaping can damage buried lines or change the areas that require coverage. Testing before and after related work helps identify changes.
