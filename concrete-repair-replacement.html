Concrete questions

What property owners commonly ask.

Should cracked concrete be repaired or replaced?

Small, stable defects may be repairable. Widespread cracking, movement, drainage problems or a failing base can make replacement the more durable choice. We evaluate the actual site before recommending either approach.

Can you match existing concrete?

New work can be coordinated with the surrounding area, but age, aggregate, sun exposure and prior treatments mean an exact color match is not always possible. We set expectations before work begins.

What affects the price?

Area, thickness, demolition, disposal, access, reinforcement, base condition, finish and drainage requirements all influence a concrete estimate.

Show us the concrete you want to repair.

Send photos, measurements and your city. We will help identify the next practical step.
